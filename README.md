# Project Title: Metagenomic exploration of MDM and FDM of fungus garden cultivated from ants with focus on novel antifungals

**Master's student**: Fábio Rodrigo de Freitas (@FabioRFreitas)

**Advisor**: Dr. Renato Augusto Corrêa dos Santos (@SantosRAC)

**External Collaborator**: Dr. Vinícius de Rezende Rodovalho (@vrrodovalho)

## **Description**: 
Microbial dark matter holds enormous, still-untapped biotechnological capacity, including for the discovery of novel antifungals. However, the bioinformatics tools widely used in characterizing metagenomes are based mainly on homology and depend on the prior characterization of similar sequences. As a result, even in known species, numerous molecules remain uncharacterized, what is termed functional dark matter. In this regard, this project aims to explore new computational approaches, especially those using Artificial Intelligence, to unravel the dark matter present in the metagenomes of fungus-farming ants, seeking to characterize previously unknown organisms and prospect for new molecules with antifungal properties, as well as to carry out comparisons between the taxonomic dark matter and the known microbial community.
